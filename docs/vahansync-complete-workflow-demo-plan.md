# VahanSync Complete Workflow Demonstration Plan

## Purpose and guardrails

This recording will demonstrate a single operational lifecycle inside a newly created, isolated VahanSync organization. It will use the live application and its real role boundaries, rather than mocked screens or fabricated outcomes. The demonstration organization will be created solely for this recording. No existing customer organization, prior QA organization, credentials, notification recipient, payment record, or production workflow will be changed.

The final result is planned as one smooth narrated product demonstration. It should show the connected operating record moving from organization setup, through fleet readiness and maintenance execution, to inventory and financial accountability. It is not intended to show unrelated hidden administrative utilities or make performance, telematics, GPS, or delivery-channel claims that the application does not currently support.

## Single demonstration story

The story follows one VIN-first coach that enters a new organization’s fleet register. Its scheduled tyre component reaches a service threshold after an authorized driver odometer update. The Fleet Manager converts that signal into a work order, the assigned Mechanic completes it with evidence and a controlled part reservation, the Inventory Manager records the resulting stock and supplier context, the Accountant observes the approved INR trace, and the Superadmin closes the loop through organization-level governance.

| Chapter | Role | Real application workflow to record | Result shown to the viewer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Establish the organization | Superadmin | Create the isolated organization, complete profile details, open the command center, and invite the role members. | A governed, role-based workspace begins with one accountable organization record. |
| 2. Define people and control | Superadmin | Review the Team workspace, accepted member roles, notifications, compliance view, billing test context where applicable, and profile/sign-out controls. | The organization owns who can act and what each role can see. |
| 3. Build the fleet record | Fleet Manager | Create a VIN-first vehicle, assign the Driver, add a lifecycle component with installation reading, expected life, and alert threshold. | Vehicle identity, ownership, and maintenance context stay connected. |
| 4. Prepare controlled stock | Inventory Manager | Create the needed inventory part, create a supplier record, and create or receive a draft purchase order as relevant to the available UI. | Parts and supplier controls exist before repair demand is fulfilled. |
| 5. Operate from the route | Driver | Open the assigned vehicle, submit the daily odometer update, complete a safety inspection or issue report, and show the updated vehicle context. | A driver action becomes an operational signal on the actual assigned vehicle. |
| 6. Dispatch the decision | Fleet Manager | Review the resulting component or maintenance signal, create the work order, set priority and notes, assign the available Mechanic, and show the maintenance queue. | A condition becomes an accountable, vehicle-scoped work order. |
| 7. Execute the repair | Mechanic | Open the assigned work order, start work, complete the checklist, reserve the approved inventory part, record repair notes and evidence, and submit completion. | The repair handoff records its labor, proof, and parts context. |
| 8. Control the handoff | Inventory Manager | Review the movement or receipt context and show the updated part availability, supplier or purchase-order context, and any stock-control action permitted by the role. | The same maintenance record drives inventory accountability. |
| 9. Close the INR trace | Accountant | Open the ledger, relevant maintenance-linked financial entry, reconciliation/export view, and permitted reversal-safe controls without Superadmin approval actions. | Finance sees the controlled operating cost without a separate spreadsheet. |
| 10. Govern and conclude | Superadmin | Return to the command center, review the work-order outcome, notification/compliance or audit context, role directory, and organization-wide signals. | Leadership sees the governed end-to-end result while role boundaries remain intact. |

## Recording structure

The browser walkthrough will use short intentional pauses at completed action states, keep each role workspace in its natural navigation order, and avoid frantic page switching. Each chapter will include a role label, one brief narration cue, and a visible final state before moving to the next responsible member. The recording will use one role session at a time and will sign out between roles when required, so no screen implies that one member can bypass another role’s permissions.

The first two existing narrated marketing clips may remain as a short opening if they fit the final pacing. The browser-recorded chapters will form the main body. A final assembly pass will provide a continuous voiceover, concise captions, smooth cuts, and a single accessible transcript.

## Completion criteria

The demonstration is complete only when the recording shows a real isolated organization, accepted role members, one assigned vehicle, one component-driven signal, a dispatched work order, a recorded mechanic completion, visible controlled inventory context, a linked finance view, and a final Superadmin governance view. Any operational action that cannot be completed in the current role UI will be described accurately and omitted rather than simulated.
