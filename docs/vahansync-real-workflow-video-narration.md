# VahanSync Real Workflow Video Narration

This narration accompanies the recorded isolated tenant workflow. It describes only actions and outcomes visibly established during the live demonstration. The final video remains approval-gated and does not replace the existing public preview until explicitly approved.

| Chapter | Recorded evidence | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Governance context | Superadmin Command Center and Team | “VahanSync begins with clear operating ownership. The Superadmin sees one connected organization, seven assigned role boundaries, active fleet signals, and the governance controls that keep every action accountable.” |
| 2. Fleet control | Fleet Manager vehicle, component, and work-order surfaces | “The Fleet Manager works from the VIN-linked fleet record. Vehicle identity, component lifecycle, current odometer, and maintenance dispatch remain connected in one operational view.” |
| 3. Dispatch | Fleet Manager creates the controlled route-readiness work order | “When route readiness needs attention, the manager creates a focused work order, selects the vehicle, adds the repair brief, sets priority, and assigns the right technician. The handoff is now traceable.” |
| 4. Technical execution | Technician starts work, records labor and checklist, then submits completion | “The Technician receives only assigned work. The execution record captures labor, repair notes, safety confirmation, diagnosis, quality checks, and a controlled handoff for manager review.” |
| 5. Approval | Fleet Manager approves the submitted handoff | “Back with the Fleet Manager, the completed handoff is reviewed and approved. The maintenance record closes with its operational, inventory, and financial context intact.” |
| 6. Driver safety | Driver updates odometer and submits passing DVIR | “The Driver closes the field loop. A trusted odometer update and a passing pre-trip inspection keep the VIN-linked vehicle record current before the next route.” |
| 7. Parts and procurement | Inventory Manager inventory, supplier, and purchase-order surfaces | “Inventory stays connected to the repair. The Inventory Manager can see the controlled tyre stock, supplier relationship, and received purchase order that replenishes the same organization ledger.” |
| 8. Finance close | Accountant ledger entry and reconciliation | “The Accountant turns operational activity into controlled INR records. The ledger ties fuel, maintenance parts, and reconciliation evidence back to the vehicle and the work that caused the cost.” |
| 9. Governance closeout | Superadmin Compliance vault, P&L analytics, Billing, and Profile | “Finally, the Superadmin returns to governance: compliance, P-and-L insight, subscription capacity, team access, and profile controls. VahanSync connects every responsible role without giving anyone more access than their work requires.” |

## Direction for Voiceover

Use **English (India)** with a clear, calm documentary delivery at a steady conversational pace. Keep transitions deliberate and concise; use brief pauses between role handoffs. Do not name or reveal email addresses, passwords, invitation links, tokens, or internal identifiers. Vehicle registration, VIN, controlled INR amounts, and role labels are operational demonstration context and should be shown only where needed for the workflow story.
