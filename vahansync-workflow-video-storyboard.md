# VahanSync Workflow Video — Landing Page Storyboard

**Purpose:** Give prospective Indian fleet operators a clear, credible introduction to VahanSync in under one minute. The video must explain the connected operating flow rather than make unverified performance claims.

**Format:** 16:9, approximately 50 seconds, English narration with an Indian accent, on-screen English captions, VahanSync navy/teal/signal-orange visual language, and no customer testimonials, pricing claims, GPS claims, or references to unlaunched live integrations.

| Time | Visual direction | Narration and matching caption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Dawn over an Indian fleet depot; route-line signal graphic resolves into the VahanSync mark. | “Keeping a fleet moving depends on seeing the next responsibility before it becomes a roadside interruption.” |
| 6–15s | Vehicle identity card with VIN, registration, current odometer and component life indicators; one tyre threshold turns amber. | “VahanSync connects vehicle identity, odometer readings, component life, and operating records in one accountable workspace.” |
| 15–25s | The amber component signal becomes a work order; a Fleet Manager assigns a Mechanic with vehicle context and priority. | “When a condition needs action, the signal becomes a traceable work order—assigned with the right vehicle context and next step.” |
| 25–35s | Mechanic completes work; approved part moves out of the inventory ledger; stock indicator updates. | “Maintenance, parts, and completion evidence stay connected, so inventory and repair decisions are based on the same record.” |
| 35–44s | Superadmin overview shows notifications, compliance status, organization controls and INR financial traceability. | “Each role sees the decisions that belong to them, while leaders retain a governed view of the organization.” |
| 44–52s | Clean VahanSync interface-style route network; closing call to action. | “VahanSync. Fleet intelligence for India’s operators. Start with one accountable signal.” |

## Narration Prompt

Speak in English with a clear, warm Indian accent and a confident, measured business-documentary tone. Leave short natural pauses between workflow stages; avoid sales-announcer exaggeration: Keeping a fleet moving depends on seeing the next responsibility before it becomes a roadside interruption. [short pause] VahanSync connects vehicle identity, odometer readings, component life, and operating records in one accountable workspace. [short pause] When a condition needs action, the signal becomes a traceable work order—assigned with the right vehicle context and next step. [short pause] Maintenance, parts, and completion evidence stay connected, so inventory and repair decisions are based on the same record. [short pause] Each role sees the decisions that belong to them, while leaders retain a governed view of the organization. [short pause] VahanSync. Fleet intelligence for India’s operators. Start with one accountable signal.
