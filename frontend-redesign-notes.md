# VahanSync Frontend Product Redesign Notes

- The public landing page remains a distinct marketing surface and continues to present VahanSync’s route-and-signal brand language.
- The authenticated application shell is being redesigned independently so role-based operational work remains focused, VIN-first, and compatible with the existing Supabase and tRPC contracts.
- The redesign will preserve every allowed role workspace from `workspaceAccess.ts`; it will change hierarchy, navigation, responsive presentation, and interaction affordances only.
- The local Inventory Manager view was checked with real tenant data after the shared visual layer was applied. Its role navigation, parts controls, stock summary, purchase-order state, and movement history remained intact while receiving the new command-surface hierarchy.
- The shared home shell now limits the protected activity-feed request to roles that are authorized for it, preventing constrained Inventory Manager and Accountant screens from initiating that avoidable request.
- The local Fleet Manager session was restored with the isolated Workflow Lab tenant and real operational data. The redesigned shell retained its strict Fleet Manager navigation, fleet-readiness workspace, live vehicle count, maintenance performance, driver handoff, work-order board, and VIN-first register context.
- The Fleet Manager Vehicles surface now presents a grouped VIN-first intake with identity, operating context, and maintenance-baseline sections, followed by a readable live register of all ten vehicle identities, route/depot context, current odometer, status, edit control, and driver handoff.
- The Fleet Manager Components surface now presents a three-stage lifecycle intake for component identity, installation evidence, and alert policy. Its real-data lifecycle coverage board continues to show the serviced Left Tire and the organisation’s preventive component schedules with remaining-life status.
- The Fleet Manager vehicle register’s driver handoff selector now reads from the permitted operational roster. The live Workspace Lab Driver is available in the selector without relying on the restricted Superadmin team directory query.
- The Fleet Manager Work orders surface remained fully functional with the redesigned product system: it retains the VIN-aware dispatcher, live eligible mechanic roster, priority and repair brief controls, queue filters, bulk handoff tools, and an accurate completed tire-service row.
- The isolated Driver role now presents the field execution workflow as a compact route-integrity surface: assigned vehicle, readiness, odometer, and saved-draft signals lead the screen, followed by clear action blocks for safety disposition, manual odometer, DVIR, issue escalation, and fuel logging. The existing single-vehicle role boundary and action controls remained intact.
- The isolated Accountant role now presents an INR finance workspace that prioritises the completed VIN-first maintenance handoff, reconciliation, per-vehicle P&L, record entry, and ledger export. The verified ₹9,500 maintenance-parts and ₹900 toll records remain clearly traceable. The existing approval-queue request still reports its pre-existing load error and remains tracked separately rather than being visually hidden.
- The isolated Inventory Manager role now presents the parts-and-supply workflow with stock, reorder, value, and purchase-order signals at the top. The real-data interface retains the three-part catalog, safe receipt/issue/transfer/adjustment controls, draft purchase order, and the completed tire-service ISSUE movement without expanding its role permissions.
- The isolated Mechanic role now presents a repair-execution workspace with the receive–repair–close operating rhythm, task queue, completed tire-service labor evidence, and service-component context. Its restricted navigation and existing assigned-work actions remain unchanged.
- The committed frontend-only redesign was released through GitHub and Vercel. The production Inventory Manager smoke check loaded the redesigned role shell, live three-part catalog, seven-on-hand Bus Tire position, draft purchase order, and maintenance-derived ISSUE movement successfully.
